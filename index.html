  • Government · Backend · AI

    Tens of thousands of records had job titles written as free text — several languages, typos, abbreviations, blanks. The same job spelled a hundred ways. All of it had to map onto one official list.

    Instead of writing rules, I let the data decide. Every record with a confirmed title casts a vote, and a new one is matched against the records whose fields line up. Weak evidence is ranked as weak instead of hidden, and nothing is ever written automatically — a human accepts it, and that acceptance becomes a vote for next time.

    91.6% precision, measured by hiding 7,021 real titles and letting it run blind.
